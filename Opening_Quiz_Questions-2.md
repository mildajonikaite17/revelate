# Opening HR Challenge — Questions

## A — Public-Record Owner: Organisation and Professional Identity

1. **[All roles; any sector]** Which of these best describes what **[organisation or practice]** does?

2. **[Private-sector employee; any hierarchy]** Which phrase appears in **[organisation]**’s published mission?

3. **[Executive or founder; verified public history]** Which happened first: **[verified public milestone A]** or **[verified public milestone B]**?

4. **[Sales or client-facing role]** Which audience is **[publicly listed product or service]** designed for?

5. **[Product or engineering]** Which problem does **[publicly documented product]** address?

6. **[HR or manager]** Which of these is one of **[organisation]**’s published values?

7. **[Public-sector employee]** Which of these services is listed on **[institution]**’s official website?

8. **[Enterprise or distributed organisation; public directory available]** Which published business unit or service area is associated with **[publicly listed offering]**?

9. **[Freelancer or independent professional]** Which specialism is demonstrated in **[public portfolio project]**, according to its published description?

10. **[Any role; limited public information]** In this fictional welcome-day story, the introduction comes first, the tour second, and the quiz third. What happens immediately before the quiz?

## B — Governance Evaluator: Clear Scenarios and Defined Rules

11. **[Product or sales]** A fictional customer needs **[need stated in the vignette]**. Which publicly documented capability of **[product]** best matches that need?

12. **[Engineering or technical specialist]** According to **[public documentation excerpt]**, which of these two approaches supports **[stated requirement]**?

13. **[HR or learning]** According to **[public careers-page excerpt]**, which advertised programme is intended for **[stated audience]**?

14. **[Manager or coordinator; public directory available]** A fictional enquiry concerns **[routine, non-personal request]**. Which service in **[public directory]** handles that type of enquiry?

15. **[Finance or operations; public guide available]** According to **[public customer guide]**, which step follows **[published step]**?

16. **[Marketing or communications]** Which of these headlines accurately summarises **[publicly announced initiative]**?

17. **[Public-sector employee]** A fictional resident needs **[public service]**. Which publicly listed service should they select from the options shown?

18. **[Freelancer or independent professional]** A fictional brief asks for a one-page summary, with no presentation. Which deliverable matches the brief: a one-page document, a slide deck, or a video?

19. **[Any role; limited public information]** In this fictional exercise, urgent requests are handled before routine requests. One urgent request and one routine request arrive together. Which should be handled first?

20. **[Manager or individual contributor; fictional teamwork puzzle]** In this fictional project, the draft must be reviewed before publication. The draft is complete but has not been reviewed. What is the next required step?

## C — Verification-First Recipient: Short, Single-Source Questions After Verification

21. **[All roles; any sector]** According to the official source excerpt shown above, what is the organisation’s main service?

22. **[Private-sector employee]** Which product name appears in **[official product-page excerpt]**?

23. **[Public-sector employee]** Which geographic area does **[institution]** serve, according to **[official source excerpt]**?

24. **[Engineering or technical specialist]** Does **[public FAQ excerpt]** list support for **[named capability]**: yes, no, or not stated?

25. **[Product or sales]** Who is **[publicly documented product or programme]** intended for, according to the source shown?

26. **[HR, manager, or new joiner]** Which learning opportunity is explicitly listed in **[public careers-page excerpt]**?

27. **[Finance or operations; public guide available]** What is the first step listed in **[public service-guide excerpt]**?

28. **[Marketing or communications]** Which statement is directly supported by **[official announcement excerpt]**?

29. **[Freelancer or independent professional]** Which service is explicitly listed in **[public practice-profile excerpt]**?

30. **[Any role; limited public information]** This fictional exercise has three steps: read the brief, prepare a draft, review the draft. Which step comes second?

## Optional Preferences — Unscored; Any Behavioural Segment

31. **[Any role]** For the next round, would you prefer a quick quiz, a short scenario, or a simple puzzle?

32. **[Any role]** Which topic would you find most interesting: organisation history, products and services, or general professional knowledge?

33. **[Any role]** Would you prefer the answer explanation after each question or in a recap at the end?

34. **[Any role]** Would you like the public source shown beside each question?

35. **[Any role]** Would you prefer a general organisation round or a round focused on your professional field?

36. **[Any role]** How did you find the opening round: too easy, about right, or too difficult?
