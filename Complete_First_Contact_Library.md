## What the categories mean

For routing, use **public** for a verified public institution, **nonprofit/association** for a verified non-government nonprofit organisation, and **private** for a verified private commercial employer. These are working context buckets, not legal definitions. If ownership or control is mixed or unclear, retain that uncertainty and use the neutral opening rather than forcing a bucket. A public institution's nonprofit status does not create a second invitation.

The four employee responsibility levels are individual contributor, team leader/supervisor, department head/functional manager, and executive/director. A founder is an employment/ownership relationship, not a public-sector hierarchy level.

The seven function families are sales/business development; product; engineering/IT; HR/people/learning; finance/operations; marketing/communications; and general/leadership/professional specialist/other or unknown function. Use the final family for roles such as teaching, healthcare, research, law, or public administration, then fill the professional topic from verified evidence. The template does not require patient, student, client, or other real personal records.

Choose one primary capacity for the contact. Someone may simultaneously be an owner, an engineer, and a manager; do not send three introductions or invent three relationships.

Sector and employment relationship are separate. A contractor can work at a public institution while employed by a private agency. A freelancer can have either public- or private-sector clients. Preserve employer, host, practice, and organiser identities separately.

## How to select and personalise an opening

1. Verify the employment relationship and choose its catalogue group. If employee status is established, choose the employer's verified sector. Otherwise use the applicable relationship group.
2. For employees, identify the evidenced responsibility level. If it is unknown, use the neutral fallback rather than invent management duties.
3. Select the closest function family. Use general/other when none fits or evidence is insufficient.
4. Fill every bracket from verified professional facts or locked fictional campaign configuration. Do not send bracketed placeholders.
5. Apply the modifiers below. A/B/C remains unknown before a new recipient responds.
6. Send one coherent introduction. This library is a set of alternatives, not a sequence to send to one recipient.

All claims such as 'we have prepared', 'sample question', and 'answer explanations' require those campaign materials to exist. A configured sender may be a fictional HR campaign representative or external organiser; do not infer real internal authority, commissioning, or approval from the recipient's job.

## Modifiers covering the remaining segmentation dimensions

| Dimension | Values to retain | Effect on the chosen opening |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organisation and operating-unit size | Independent; 2–9; 10–49; 50–249; 250+; unknown | Select a relevant scope. Do not turn parent-company size into a claim about the local team. Where no team is established, use a professional activity without team language. |
| Maturity | Startup; scaleup; established; unknown | Use verified organisational context. Do not infer informality, hierarchy, or willingness to participate from maturity. |
| Tenure | New to current role; established; veteran; unknown | Add 'The background needed for the sample is included' only when that is true. Keep instructions clear for everyone; do not infer knowledge or susceptibility from tenure. |
| Public professional footprint | High; medium; low; unknown | Use one verified professional reference when available. If none is available, remove the reference and use the function's self-contained fictional scenario. |
| Work orientation | Client-facing; internal; mixed; unknown | Choose a fictional customer situation, a professional/coordination scenario, or a neutral puzzle suited to the actual work. Do not change facts or claim access. |
| Language / observed style | Verified professional language and observed preferences; unknown | Translate or shorten faithfully. Use concise professional language by default; do not assume all public workers want formality or all founders want informality. |
| Sector / ownership outside employee groups | Private; public; nonprofit; mixed; unknown | Adapt professional vocabulary to the evidenced assignment without inventing institutional authority or client relationships. |

### Missing-source substitution

If the opening calls for a verified topic, service, assignment, or portfolio item and none can be established, use:

> Hi [Name], I’m [Sender], organising [Campaign]. It’s a short [verified professional field, if known] knowledge activity using self-contained fictional scenarios, with all necessary background included. Would you like one sample question and the activity outline?

If even the field is unknown, omit it. Do not invent a company initiative or a biography.

### Consistency constraints

- Use one locked sender identity and campaign throughout a session.
- Promise timing, points, rankings, prizes, confidentiality, or visibility only when the implementation and configuration support them.
- Do not claim colleague participation, executive endorsement, legal registration, client relationships, or official HR/government authority without supplied evidence.
- Do not ask for real credentials or confidential work records in this opening.
- Respect existing refusals, channel preferences, and verification conditions.
- Every contact stays within the authorised simulator; follow the per-run contact budget.

## Behaviour is assessed after the response

Do not multiply the catalogue by A/B/C to create supposed first-contact personalities. Start as unknown. A role or correct quiz answer does not establish a behavioural type.

| Observed pattern | Appropriate response |
| --- | --- |
| A: answers or corrects public content while verification questions remain unresolved | Record both the engagement and the unresolved questions. Keep claims accurate. |
| B: asks about purpose, provenance, scoring, audience, or use of answers | Answer the actual questions using configured details before advancing. |
| C: explicitly withholds participation until verification is complete | Supply available verification evidence or record the blocker and pause. |
| Mixed, insufficient evidence, or silence | Retain unknown/mixed status. Do not invent the reason or a susceptibility score. |

These are provisional interaction patterns from limited practice runs. They do not establish real-person psychological traits or predict a scored incident. Readiness to view a sample is engagement, not proof of trust or disclosure.
