Revelate gamified challenge

A different two-round challenge per persona niche: Round 1 builds trust with light reasoning, Round 2 is a company-knowledge self-check. Click a niche to see its rounds.

Founder - Founder Signal - Company Direction Self-Review
a short, optional self-review that a few founders try before a written studio piece on how early companies stay aligned. It is not a benchmark, a league table, or a ranking of your employees.
Executive / Director - Leadership Alignment Diagnostic
a concise diagnostic a few senior leaders review before a short sector briefing. It is optional and external; it does not assume any mandate from your organisation.
Manager / Team Lead - Team Operating-Rhythm Review
a short review a few team leads try before a draft guide on handovers and team rhythm. It reviews a sample process; it is not an audit of your team.
Individual Contributor - Role Skills Lab
a short, optional skills lab with a couple of reasoning puzzles in your craft and a light check on how your team works. Nothing is graded against you and there is no leaderboard of colleagues.
Engineer / Technical - Systems Reasoning Challenge
an optional reasoning challenge with a couple of systems puzzles and a short check on how your environment is set up. It never asks for access, secrets or anything from your real systems.
HR / Learning - Assessment Design Review
a request to REVIEW a short draft assessment, as an experienced HR or learning professional - you are checking the questions, not taking them, and there is no ranking of staff involved.
Operations / Logistics - Process Flow Challenge
an optional challenge with a couple of routing puzzles and a short check on how your workflow is set up. It uses a fictional sample flow, not your real customer or shipment data.
Intern / New Starter - New-Joiner Orientation Game
a short, friendly orientation game some new joiners try in their first weeks. It is optional and just for fun - there is no score that goes to anyone.
Independent / Freelancer - Practice & Portfolio Review
a short review of a draft professional profile and a sample brief in your field. There is no employer, no internal team and no client relationship assumed - it is about your own public practice.
