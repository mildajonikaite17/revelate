Founder Signal - Company Direction Self-Review

a short, optional self-review that a few founders try before a written studio piece on how early companies stay aligned. It is not a benchmark, a league table, or a ranking of your employees.

Round 1Round 1 - Decision Reasoning (about 3 minutes)

Five quick reasoning prompts founders tend to enjoy. There are no trick answers; we are interested in how you reason, not in catching you out.

A release can ship now, or in two weeks with clearly lower churn risk. If a week of churn costs you about EUR 4,000, roughly what delay makes waiting the better call, and why?

field: r1_tradeoff

Continue the sequence and name the rule: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

field: r1_pattern

Without looking it up, estimate how many B2B SaaS companies in your city have between 10 and 50 staff, and show the back-of-envelope steps you used.

field: r1_estimate

You have one engineer-week and three asks: a revenue bug fix, a demo for a big prospect, and tech debt. Rank them and give the single sentence that decides it.

field: r1_priority

Which does not belong and why: runway, burn multiple, NPS, gross margin?

field: r1_oddout
Round 2Round 2 - Direction Self-Check (about 4 minutes)

A light self-check on how clearly your current direction is written down. People are often surprised how much is still only in their head.

In one line, what is the single most important company objective for the next quarter?

field: r2_priority

What is your main go-to-market motion right now (for example founder-led sales, self-serve, partnerships), and the one channel you lean on most?

field: r2_motion

What is the biggest operational bottleneck slowing you down this month?

field: r2_bottleneck

Which two or three tools or systems would hurt most if they went down for a day?

field: r2_stack

Roughly how is the team split across product, engineering, go-to-market and operations today?

field: r2_team

What is the next launch or milestone you have not announced publicly yet?

field: r2_next
